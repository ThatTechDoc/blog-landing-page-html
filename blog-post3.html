The Unexpected Journey

April 25, 2025

Life has a way of surprising us, sometimes taking us down paths we never imagined. Not all detours are detours; some are divine redirections.

This post tells the story of one such journey—how a canceled opportunity led to personal growth, new friendships, and a clearer purpose. It reminded me that not getting what I wanted was exactly what I needed.

I once thought I knew where I was going. My plans were set, my goals mapped out. And then—life laughed. Detours showed up. Doors closed. Others opened.

“Sometimes the wrong train takes you to the right station.” – Paulo Coelho

At first, I resisted the change. But looking back, the shift was exactly what I needed. Growth hides in the unexpected.

Now, I hold my plans loosely and walk with curiosity. I trust the journey, even when I don’t understand it.
